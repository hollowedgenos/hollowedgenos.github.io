## Specifics
Each scene is intended to contribute to the over all feel and narrative of Well Well Wells. The player traverses different locales on their journey to the end of Level 1. 

Some scenes pose a challenge that the player must overcome, such as finding their way through a maze, or completing a section of the Level using platforming elements.
