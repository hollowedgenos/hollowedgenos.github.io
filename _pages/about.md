<span style="font-family: 'Calibri Light', serif;">My name is Connor Touchet, also known as HollowedGenos! I create game systems and aim to become a game systems programmer who pushes the envelope for what game systems can do for players. As a game designer, novel, fresh, and practical ideas are what motivate me. I gravitate toward mechanics and systems which actively engage and/or challenge the player. Being able to convey compelling gameplay and storytelling are why I create games. I want to inspire others in the industry to challenge themselves to make more games that dare to stand out.</span>

<span style="font-family: 'Calibri Light', serif;">My main philosophy pertaining to game design centers around a single question: "Wouldn't it be cool if?", and further iterating upon this premise. When generating ideas, I set out to answer questions relating to a game system or mechanic and its relationship with the player. What does this mechanic focus on? Is it necessary? Is it intriguing? Is it fun? Would emphasizing it in the narrative add value to it? Do I have the know-how to implement it and how long would it take to do so? Once I have assessed an idea as worth pursuing, I write the pseudocode for it on a piece of paper or a text document. This step helps me organize my thoughts and acts as a guide during the implementation process. The prototyping stage is done in my game engine of choice, Godot, though I am no stranger to prototyping systems in Unity. Putting these design methods into practice, I created the networked inventory for the Soulsync project C# in Unity Engine. One of the main focuses primarily on maintaining data persistence between the host and clients. I also was responsible for the simple but practical typewriter effect for a dialogue user interface in Godot using GDScript.</span>

<span style="font-family: 'Calibri Light', serif;">I aim to leverage my design philosophy in the game industry to create truly unique gameplay experiences, which enthrall and captivate players. I aspire to translate my passion for the medium into gameplay systems and mechanics that are memorable, just as they are meaningful. I believe that an inquisitive mind can break down systems to their base elements, finding out what makes them fun to interact with. By breaking my systems down to their base elements, I aim to architect mechanics that are as robust as they are memorable.</span>
